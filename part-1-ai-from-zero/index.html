    🐣 Part I · AI from Zero¶

    Brand new to AI? You're in exactly the right place. This part assumes nothing: what AI is, how chatbots work, your very first conversation, how to ask good questions, how to stay safe, and a gentle 30-day plan that turns you into a confident everyday user. No jargon without an explanation, no judgment, and lots of little wins along the way. 🌱

    ✅ Key Points & Steps

    This part takes you from never having used an AI chatbot to using one confidently every day. No technical background is needed.

    • Learn the basics: what AI is, how chatbots produce their answers, and how to set up your first assistant.
    • Get better results: how to write clear requests, follow up, and use voice, photos and files.
    • Use it wisely: how to spot mistakes, protect your privacy, and build a simple daily habit over 30 days.

    💡 You can't break anything

    The single most important thing to know as a beginner: you can't hurt the AI and you can't break it. Ask "silly" questions, make typos, start over as many times as you like. Everyone who's good at this got good by playing. 💜
